Sasilka Day Care Center

Care for the elderly.

Sasilka is a place where the elderly can come for meals, company, and cheerful activity during the day, then go home each evening with a smile and a joyful heart.

Get Involved Our Story

Why a Day Centre for the Elderly?

This world is a beautiful but temporary place, and with age, the future becomes more uncertain. Our elders have lived through different challenges and have coped with all that life has thrown at them. While there is the extended family and community support, times are hard and there is limited to non-existed Care Centre in Nigeria as a whole and less chances of having such in smaller towns and villages. Sasilka Empowerment Initiative has been blessed with a good heart and a passion to support and give. This is her way of giving back to the community. Sasilka Day Centre is intended as a free continuous, flowing, and on-going charity in memory of the Zakama and Amina Herman Pilasar.

Impacts of Sasilka On Community

Through its activities, Sasilka Day Care Centre has created a regular point of connection for elderly persons from different communities in Garkida. The Centre provides not only food but also companionship and a sense of belonging.

Our regular feeding activities are particularly important because food insecurity and increasing living costs can make it difficult for vulnerable elderly persons to access adequate and nutritious meals consistently.

Our Beneficiaries

The Centre currently serves approximately 100 elderly members from different communities in and around Garkida. Our beneficiaries come together at the Centre to receive meals, interact with one another, participate in social activities, and enjoy an environment that promotes dignity, acceptance, and emotional well-being.

Partner with Sasilka

Sasilka Empowerment Initiative welcomes partnerships with government institutions, development organisations, philanthropic organisations, private-sector organisations, community leaders, and individuals who share our commitment to improving the welfare of elderly and vulnerable persons.

We particularly seek support for food supplies, nutrition, healthcare initiatives, recreational activities, and other programmes that directly improve the quality of life of our elderly beneficiaries.
