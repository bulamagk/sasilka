Our Core Areas of Support

Sasilka Day Care Centre focuses on:

  1. Food and Nutrition: Providing regular meals and refreshments to elderly members.
  2. Social Interaction: Creating opportunities for companionship and meaningful interaction.
  3. Emotional Well-being: Providing an environment where elderly members feel loved, accepted, and valued.
  4. Dignity and Inclusion: Promoting respect and dignity for older persons within the community.
  5. Community Support: Responding to the needs of vulnerable elderly persons through community-based care.
  6. Recreation and Engagement: Providing activities that encourage social participation and a sense of purpose.
